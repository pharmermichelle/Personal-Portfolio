How to Play

Move pieces left or right and rotate them to fill entire rows. Completed rows break apart and disappear. Clear 3 rows per level to reach the next corrupted sector.

Keyboard: ← → to move, ↑ to rotate, ↓ to soft drop, Space to hard drop, P to pause. Mobile: use the five buttons immediately below the board.
