⚔️ How to play

Pick up to five different cats. Press Start level. Every cat type marches out automatically according to its own timer.

Defeat dogs to earn 🌿 catnip immediately. Tap Boost to spend it on temporary Power, Speed, Lives or Spawn upgrades for your squad. Catnip starts at zero each level. 🐟 Fish coins are saved for permanent upgrades between levels.

Tap the dog castle on the battlefield or its health card to inspect enemy stats. Dogs march out as occasional single scouts and slower, stronger groups. There is no final wave to wait for: when cats reach the kennel and no dogs remain, they can damage its gate immediately. 🐾 markers on kennel health show when its guardians appear.

Castle gates take controlled strikes rather than instant damage from every cat standing nearby.

🔎 Easier viewing

Use ⛶ to enlarge the battlefield, or turn your phone sideways for a wider battle view. The battle pauses while a menu is open. At level 5, dog bosses defend their castle once its health falls below 65%; tap Summon to deploy a purchased legendary cat.

Start Over resets coins, cat unlocks, heroes, upgrades, levels, and battle catnip. It asks you to confirm before deleting your current progress.
