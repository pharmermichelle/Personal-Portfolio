How to explore

Mobile: Use the four arrows below the room, or tap a cabinet to walk toward it. Press Play when you reach it.

Desktop: Move with arrow keys or WASD. Press E or Enter near a cabinet to open it.

Your Arcade Identity

Signed-in players appear as their saved avatar, with equipped hockey wearables where available. Guest players get a starter character. Your game XP is awarded inside the games, not by walking around.
